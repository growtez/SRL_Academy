Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding our One-to-One Coaching Fees and services

Why are your One-to-One Coaching Fees so affordable?

We believe in democratizing education. By removing the overhead costs of physical classrooms and large-scale marketing, we pass those savings directly to you. Our goal is to provide One-to-One Coaching that is sustainable for every family while maintaining the highest quality of tutors.

Can I choose a specific tutor for my one-on-one sessions?

Yes. Depending on your subject requirements and budget (ranging from ₹300/h to ₹1000/h), you can select from our pool of IITians, NITians, MBBS doctors, and PhD holders. We provide profiles of our tutors so you can choose the mentor who best fits your learning style.

What is included in the Course Fees for a single subject?

When you opt for a subject-wise Course package, you receive:

  • Complete syllabus coverage for that specific subject (Physics, Chemistry, Maths or Biology).
  • Personalized one-on-one live sessions.
  • Targeted problem-solving and doubt-clearing.
  • Access to experienced faculty with up to 25 years of teaching experience.
Is there a minimum number of hours I need to book?

No, one of the best parts of our One-to-One Coaching structure is the flexibility. You can book hours as you need them. However, most students find that a consistent weekly schedule yields the best results for rank improvement.

Do you offer a trial class before I pay the fees?

Absolutely. We encourage all students to take a demo session. This ensures you are comfortable with the tutor's teaching methodology and our online platform before committing to any coaching fees.
